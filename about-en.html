Water Knowledge Hub

About us

Water is the source of life and a sustainable future

This platform is being developed to bring together information on the Republic of Uzbekistan’s water resources, water management system and environmental sustainability in one place.

The platform’s main purpose is to give the public reliable, accessible information on the responsible use and protection of water resources, as well as on government programs and research projects underway in the water sector.

Explore the hub

Our goal

To provide comprehensive information on the state of Uzbekistan’s water resources, government reforms, modern water-saving technologies and environmental challenges.

The platform is for students, educators, researchers and water professionals. It supports sharing experience and building partnerships in education and research.

Government policy, strategies and reforms

The platform provides information on policy frameworks, national strategies, government programs and relevant legal and regulatory instruments adopted by the Republic of Uzbekistan for water resources management and development of the water sector.

It also covers ongoing government reforms and priorities for efficient use of water resources, adoption of water-saving technologies, modernization of irrigation and land reclamation systems, environmental sustainability and restoration of the Aral Sea region.

The platform presents information on government policy, strategic goals and ongoing projects in the water sector in a clear, structured format for the general public, students, researchers and specialists.

Our main areas of work

  • Provide information about Uzbekistan’s water resources and their condition.

  • Report on government programs and projects in the water sector.

  • Cover the Aral Sea crisis and ecological restoration efforts in the surrounding region.

  • Introduce water-saving technologies and innovative solutions.

  • Compile research and statistical data.

Our approach

We combine reliable information, a scientific approach and modern technology to build knowledge and raise awareness about protecting water resources.

Protecting water means protecting the future!
