Our purpose

Our goal is to make knowledge about water management, irrigation and conservation accessible to a wide audience. The platform offers learning materials and practical examples from Uzbekistan.

The platform is for students, educators, researchers and water professionals. It supports sharing experience and building partnerships in education and research.
